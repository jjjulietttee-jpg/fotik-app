Fotik Get the App

Swipe to clean your photo library

Fotik is an iPhone photo cleaner that finds duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, screen recordings, blurry photos and oversized videos, then lets you sort them by swiping: left to delete, right to keep, up to add to Favorites. Analysis happens on your device, there are no ads, and it shows honestly that deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days before the space comes back. Scanning and browsing are free; deleting needs Fotik Pro.

What Fotik does

  • Duplicates and similar shotsGroups identical and near-identical photos so you can choose which to keep. Matching runs on the device.
  • Screenshots and screen recordingsEach has its own group, so you can clear them without scrolling the whole camera roll.
  • Blurry and shaky photosA sharpness check flags likely blurry shots. It is an estimate; you review before deleting.
  • Large videos and large photosLists the biggest files first, since a few videos often take more space than thousands of photos.
  • Live Photos, bursts and old photosSeparate groups for Live Photos, burst series and photos older than a year.
  • Swipe sortingSwipe left to mark for deletion, right to keep, up to add to Favorites. Undo is available, and nothing is removed until you confirm Delete.
  • Honest about freed spaceiOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Fotik shows what is waiting there and what is actually freed as two separate numbers.
  • Month by month, On This Day, streaksWork through your library one month at a time, revisit On This Day photos, and keep a daily cleaning streak.

Price

Scanning your library and swiping through photos works without paying; deleting photos needs Fotik Pro. Fotik Pro costs $6.99 per week with a 3-day free trial, or $29.99 per year with a 3-day free trial (US prices), and a one-time lifetime purchase is also offered. Prices differ by country (in Poland: 29,99 zl per week, 129,99 zl per year, 169,99 zl lifetime). Cancel any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to clean photos on iPhone?

It depends on what you want. Fotik is built for people who prefer to sort by swiping and want an honest account of freed space, with no ads and on-device analysis. Cleanup has the largest user base, and Slidebox is cheaper. The comparison on each guide page lists prices and limits.

Does Fotik upload my photos?

No. Photos are analysed on the device and nothing is uploaded to a server. The app contains no ads.

Is Fotik free?

Scanning, browsing and swiping are free. Deleting photos requires Fotik Pro (weekly with a 3-day trial, yearly, or lifetime).

Why didn't my storage go down after deleting photos?

iOS moves deleted photos to Recently Deleted for 30 days and they still take space. Empty that album in the Photos app to free the space.

Can Fotik delete photos by itself?

No. You choose what to delete, and iOS asks you to confirm.

Which languages does it support?

English, Polish, German, French and Spanish (Mexico).

Does it work on iPad or Mac?

Fotik is built for iPhone.
