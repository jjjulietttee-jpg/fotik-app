How to Delete Duplicate Photos on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Fotik developer

Short answer. Open a duplicate finder, review each group and delete the extra copies. Fotik groups duplicates and similar shots, suggests which one to keep, and lets you decide by swiping before anything is deleted.

Step by step

  1. Scan the libraryOpen Fotik and allow photo access; the scan runs on your device.
  2. Open DuplicatesChoose the Duplicates group, then Similar for near-identical shots such as retakes.
  3. Review each groupFotik suggests which photo to keep. Check it and change the choice if you prefer another frame.
  4. Delete the extrasMark the extra copies and confirm Delete (requires Fotik Pro). They go to Recently Deleted.
  5. Empty Recently DeletedDelete them there in the Photos app to actually free the space.

Frequently asked questions

Are similar photos the same as duplicates?

No. Duplicates are identical or nearly identical copies; similar shots are different frames of the same scene, such as burst retakes. Fotik shows them in separate groups.

Can it delete the wrong photo?

Matching is automatic but every deletion is chosen and confirmed by you. Review each group.

Does it need internet?

Matching is done on the device and nothing is uploaded.

Does iOS have a built-in duplicate finder?

Yes. Photos has a Duplicates album in recent iOS versions that detects exact duplicates. Fotik also groups near-identical shots and adds swipe sorting.

Is it free?

Finding and reviewing is free; deleting requires Fotik Pro.

What about Live Photos and bursts?

They have separate groups, so you can handle them on their own.
