How to Free Up Space From Your iPhone Photo Library

Updated October 2026 · By the Fotik developer

Short answer. Delete the largest and most repeated files first: big videos, duplicates, screenshots and screen recordings. Fotik groups them and lets you sort by swiping, then reminds you that the space returns only after you empty Recently Deleted.

Step by step

  1. Let Fotik scanOpen Fotik and allow access to your photos. It scans the library on the device.
  2. Start with large videos and duplicatesOpen the large videos group and the duplicates group; these usually free the most space per decision.
  3. Swipe or selectSwipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it, up to favorite it. Check the number marked.
  4. Confirm DeleteTap Delete and confirm the iOS prompt. Fotik needs Pro for this step.
  5. Empty Recently DeletedOpen Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted and delete those items. Only then is the space freed.

Frequently asked questions

Which group frees the most space?

Usually large videos, because one video can weigh as much as hundreds of photos. Fotik lists the biggest files first.

Will it delete photos for me?

No. You mark photos and confirm; iOS asks for permission to delete.

Is scanning free?

Yes. Scanning and browsing are free; deleting needs Fotik Pro.

Do photos stored in iCloud free local space?

Photos whose full original is only in iCloud take little local space, so deleting them may free less than expected.

Can I recover a mistake?

Until you empty Recently Deleted (30 days), you can recover items from there in the Photos app.

Does it work without internet?

Analysis runs on the device. Subscription status may need a connection.
