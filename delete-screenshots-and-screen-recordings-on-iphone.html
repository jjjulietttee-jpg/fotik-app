How to Delete All Screenshots and Screen Recordings on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Fotik developer

Short answer. Open the Screenshots group in Fotik, swipe through them (left to delete, right to keep) and confirm. Screen recordings have their own group, which is where the big files usually are.

Step by step

  1. Open ScreenshotsAfter the scan, tap Screenshots on the home screen.
  2. Swipe throughSwipe left to mark for deletion, right to keep, up to favorite.
  3. Do Screen RecordingsOpen the Screen Recordings group; videos there often take the most space.
  4. Confirm and emptyConfirm Delete (Fotik Pro), then empty Recently Deleted in Photos to free the space.
